## Independence check (2026-10-26)
For each LOSO fold, every test-season fake outcome was flipped (hit <-> no hit) and the fold refitted. The test
predictions, the daily picks and both tier selections were identical in all four folds (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025).
The picks therefore do not depend on the outcomes they are scored on, and the 79 fake outcomes are independent
Bernoulli(0.75) draws. Redrawing them 5,000 times gives a mean of 0.751; none of the redraws was as low as 0.532.
The 8 other null seeds tested (2001-2008) gave pooled LOSO M1 top-20% rates of 0.695-0.867.

batter PA < 100: 23% vs 11%; starter with no 365-day history: 37% vs 7% of all M1 top-1 picks; indoor games: 62% vs

z = -4.48 (exact binomial one-sided p = 2.2e-5). It was noticed after scanning many numbers across several seeds, which makes
finding one tail this far out less surprising than its own p-value suggests. The 200-seed null study tests whether
such tails occur more often than chance allows.
