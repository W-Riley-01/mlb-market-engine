Beat the Streak lab, Step 1 - choices where the plan (f10661f) and addendum A1 (6d9cb3c) are silent
Builder: analysis/btslab_features.py. Table: analysis/cache/btslab_features.parquet (git-ignored).

Table shape
1. The table stores as-of COUNTS and league rates, not shrunk rates. The plan estimates the prior strength m per
   training fold (Section 4 common rules) and standardises/fills per fold, so shrinkage, standardisation, missing
   fills and the D split (which uses C's shrunk BF) are applied at fit time: estimate_m(), shrink(), pa_split()
   in the builder, with an assert self-check. Every stored count is what a leak would sit in, so the checks run on it.
2. Outcome columns ab, h are attached from the box score and were only counted (AB>=1 / AB=0).

Dates and seasons
3. Each history game is dated by the last schedule date on which it appears as Final (its "effective date"), and
   all its pitches take that date. Suspended or postponed games therefore enter history only once complete. The
   "strictly before D" rule is applied to that date; doubleheader game 2 never sees game 1.
4. History = regular-season games (schedule gameType R) 2019-2025 from data/pitch_matrix.parquet. Pool = 2022-2025
   games that are Final on their official date and never suspended (as in the v4 backtest): 9,704 games; minus
   the 18 neutral-site games (324 rows) = 174,348 rows.
5. The 2019-2021 schedules (623 day files) were fetched with backtest_v4.get_json into analysis/cache/api/schedule/;
   log analysis/out/btslab_schedule_fetch_log.txt. They are history only. Each day file was read as UTF-8 bytes.

Event definitions (as R2 Section 3 unless stated)
6. PA = pitch row with non-null events (includes truncated_pa, as in R2). H = single/double/triple/home_run.
   AB = PA minus walk, hit_by_pitch, sac_fly, sac_bunt, sac_fly_double_play, sac_bunt_double_play, catcher_interf.
   K = strikeout, strikeout_double_play. BB = walk (+ intent_walk, which never occurs: automatic intentional walks
   have no pitch, so they are absent from pitch data and BB% is slightly low). ISO numerator xb = total bases - H.

A. Batter
7. Window [D-365, D) for H, AB, PA, K, BB, xb. Platoon = the batter's H and AB in the same window against pitchers
   of the opposing starter's hand (per-PA p_throws, so switch hitters are handled by PA).
8. Starter hand = the starter's most recent p_throws strictly before D. No prior pitch -> hand unknown and the
   platoon counts are missing (0.96% of rows); the hand of the current game is not used.

C. Opposing starter (actual first pitcher, addendum A1 change 3)
9. H, AB, PA (= batters faced), K, BB, xb allowed in [D-365, D). H/AB allowed uses AB against.
10. Expected BF: sum and count of BF over the last up-to-10 starts before D, any season back to 2019. BF per
    start = box battersFaced when the game's box score is cached, else counted PAs in pitch_matrix (addendum
    change 7). League mean BF per start in [D-365, D) is stored as the shrinkage target.

D. Expected PA
11. E_PA by slot and side = PA / games of the first 9 distinct batters per team-game in [D-365, D), regular season
    only (B2's construction restricted to gameType R). The split min(E_PA, max(0, (BF_exp - slot)/9 + 1)) is
    computed at fit time.

E. Bullpen (opposing team)
12. Weights = each reliever's relief pitches for that team in [D-30, D), excluding the game's starter (as v4's
    bullpen_for). Rate = pooled sum(w*H) / sum(w*AB) of those relievers' allowed H and AB in [D-365, D): a
    pitch-weighted rate in which tiny samples weigh little. Relief pitches = box numberOfPitches when cached,
    else pitch rows.
13. Fatigue = relief pitches and distinct relievers in [D-3, D), all relievers (no starter exclusion).
14. Each team's first game of a season has no relievers in the 30-day window -> missing (0.6% of rows).

F. Park, weather, roof
15. Park counts: H and PA (both teams) in park-eligible games at the venue, and the home team's road H and PA,
    from Jan 1 of (season - 3) to D. The factor ((venue H + m*road rate)/(venue PA + m)) / road rate is formed at
    fit time; with no venue history it is 1.
16. Park-eligible excludes the 2022-2025 neutral venues (addendum list) plus Monterrey 2019 (2701), Omaha 2019
    (5365) and Bristol 2025 (6130), found in the fetched schedules, and 2020 games not at the home team's 2019 or
    2021 usual (modal) venue: 55 relocated games. Using 2021 as well keeps Texas's new Globe Life Field (2020) and
    excludes Toronto's Sahlen Field (2020). TD Ballpark (Dunedin, Toronto 2021) is a temporary home, not a neutral
    site, and keeps its own venue factor; it never appears in the pool.
17. NOT IN PLAN: Tampa Bay played 2025 at George M. Steinbrenner Field (new venue id). It is treated like
    Sacramento: its own factor, starting at 1 and built only from games there before D. Daikin Park and Rate Field
    (2025) are renamings with unchanged venue ids.
18. Temperature = degrees parsed from the box score weather text; "Roof Closed" or "Dome" -> temperature missing
    (league-mean fill at fit time) and f_indoor = 1. Wind mph parsed from the box score wind text, exploratory
    column x_wind_mph only.

G. Recent form
19. Batter H and AB in [D-14, D) and [D-30, D); shrunk toward the batter's own 365-day H/AB at fit time.

Comparators and exploratory columns
20. base_b1, base_b2 = B1 and B2 computed by v4_analysis.add_baselines exactly as in R2 (its own windows over all
    pitch_matrix rows with game_date < D, i.e. including spring/postseason as R2 did), with the actual starter.
    base_b2 is also exploratory H. BASE_max_date = latest pitch_matrix date used by those windows.
21. NOT BUILT: the R2 simulator P(hit) (exploratory H). It exists only in the ledger_v4r2 files, which Step 1 may
    not open. It can be joined by (game_pk, player id) in a later step.
22. x_mkt_team_runs = the batting team's implied runs from the closing total and de-vigged moneyline (plan 4B,
    exponent 1.83), matched as in the v4 backtest. Exploratory "M1 + market" only (addendum A1 change 4).

Same-day attributes (plan Section 3 exceptions; no max date): slot, opp_sp, venue_id, f_temp_f, f_indoor,
x_wind_mph, x_mkt_team_runs.

2026-10-26 - Bug fix in group E (bullpen), found by leak check (b); no model had been fit
- Bug: build() made one bullpen lookup per (opposing team, date, opposing starter), but summed the reliever
  weights by (team, date) only. On a doubleheader date the team has two starters, so its relievers were added
  twice: e_bp_relievers_30d doubled and e_bp_hab mixed the two games' starter exclusions. No future data was used;
  the full build and the truncated rebuild simply aggregated differently.
- Diagnosis: run 1 of check (b) had 10 mismatched cells in 7 distinct rows. All 7 were rows whose opposing team
  played two games that date; none of the 293 sampled rows without such a doubleheader mismatched. Evidence:
  analysis/out/btslab_leakcheck_log_run1_FAILED.txt.
- Fix: btslab_features.build(), group E - the reliever pairs carry the starter, and the aggregation and its merge
  are keyed by (team, date, starter), so each game gets its own lookup, counted once. Every other group-by and
  merge keyed by team and date was reviewed: daily sums of earlier dates (park road counts, relief slices,
  fatigue) are meant to include both games of an earlier doubleheader, and the rest are keyed by game_pk or
  pitcher. No other instance was found.
- Effect: the rebuild changed only e_bp_relievers_30d (5,796 rows) and e_bp_hab (1,692 rows); the 5,796 rows are
  exactly the rows whose opposing team played a doubleheader that date. All other 62 columns are identical.
- Recheck (analysis/out/btslab_leakcheck_log.txt): (a) 0 violations; (b) 300 random rows plus 150 rows from 25
  doubleheader pairs (both games, all seasons), all 64 columns, 28,800 cells, 0 mismatches; (c) 20 rows x 16
  values by plain filtering, including last-10-start BF, park counts and raw factor, and B2, 0 mismatches.
