### Summary (written 2026-10-26 from the tables above; read-only, nothing fetched)
- Source: data/master_physics_vault.parquet holds the same 4,768,010 pitches as data/pitch_matrix.parquet plus
  game_type, launch_speed, launch_angle, hc_x, hc_y. Use it (or a schedule join) for the regular-season filter;
  pitch_matrix has no game type.
- Built by data_ingestion.py: a fixed column list, and every pitch with no pitch_type or release_speed is dropped
  before saving. Pitch type is therefore 100% non-null by construction, and untracked pitches are gone.
- Expected stats (Statcast estimated_ba / estimated_woba_using_speedangle) are not stored anywhere; the ingest
  does not keep them. The project's own xwoba_est exists only for balls in play in contact_matrix_env_fixed.parquet.
- Launch speed on balls in play: 99.1-99.8% by season.
- Codes: ST (sweeper) and SV (slurve) appear from 2019 on, so Savant has retro-labelled earlier seasons; ST rises
  from 3% to 24% of breaking balls (2019 to 2025) while SL falls from 58% to 47%. Whether that is usage or labelling
  cannot be told from the data, so SL/ST/SV should be pooled.
  Proposed families: fastball FF, SI, FA, (FC); breaking SL, ST, SV, CU, KC, CS; offspeed CH, FS, FO, SC;
  other KN, EP, PO, UN. Ambiguous: FC (cutter: fastball or breaking), FS (splitter: offspeed or fastball variant),
  KC (counted as breaking), FA (generic, mostly position players), SC/FO (rare). PO is a pitchout, not a pitch.
- 2026: no pitch-level data in files, caches or Postgres. data_ingestion.py --year 2026 would request Baseball
  Savant via pybaseball.statcast in monthly chunks (Mar 15 - Nov 5), i.e. the full regular season, but pybaseball
  is not installed in either Python environment or listed in requirements.txt, and the call is untested for 2026.
- PA alignment (2022-2025): box PA = pitch-row PA - truncated_pa + intentional walks + lost PAs.
  truncated_pa (271-319 a season) are pitch rows that are not PAs; automatic intentional walks (474-555 a season)
  have no pitch row (no intent_walk event exists); 548 PAs in 276 games (2.8%) were lost to dropped untracked
  pitches, mostly one per game (254 games), but 7 games lost 8-78 PAs and 1 game has no pitch rows at all.

### What would block as-of arsenal profiles
1. No expected-stat columns: profiles based on Statcast xBA/xwOBA need a re-pull with those columns (and
   pybaseball installed); otherwise use outcomes or the project's own launch-speed/angle lookup.
2. Thin samples per family and side: a starter's offspeed pitches to one side over 365 days has median 68-87 and
   10th percentile 0-1 (PA-ending: median 17-22, p10 0). Pitcher-side profiles need shrinkage or pooling over sides.
3. Sweeper/slider labelling drift: per-code profiles (SL vs ST) are unreliable across seasons; families avoid it.
4. Dropped untracked pitches: a few games have whole stretches missing; any PA-level join must allow for PAs with
   no pitch rows (IBB, lost PAs) and pitch rows that are not PAs (truncated_pa).
5. No 2026 pitch data, so lab 2 has the same 2022-2025-only scope as the BTS lab unless 2026 is pulled.
None of these blocks building the profiles strictly before the game date: game_date, game_pk and pitch order exist
for every row.
